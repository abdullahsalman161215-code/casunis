pizza

pizza is food

• 16th Century: Tomatoes arrive in Europe from the Americas and are eventually adopted by the poor (lazzaroni) in Naples to top their yeast-based flatbreads. • 1738: Antica Pizzeria Port'Alba opens in Naples as a street-vendor stand, later evolving into the world's first true sit-down pizzeria. • 1849: Writer Emmanuele Rocco documents early pizza styles in Naples, including variants with oil, salt, garlic, and oregano (the predecessor to the marinara). • 1889: Neapolitan pizzaiolo Raffaele Esposito of Pizzeria Brandi creates the Pizza Margherita (tomato, mozzarella, and basil) for visiting Queen Margherita of Savoy, matching the colors of the Italian flag.
